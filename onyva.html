Onyva Quick Start Guide

Creating and using your packing list

Onyva helps you build a packing list for your trip in seconds, then lets you check items off as you pack. Here’s how to get started.

1. Start a new trip

Tap New Trip and enter:

  • Name your trip — Annual Conference, Beach Weekend.
  • Destination — start typing a city name and select it from the autocomplete suggestions.
  • Travel dates — when you're leaving and returning.

2. Get your packing list

Select a packing template. Onyva comes with starter templates, but you can create your own by going to the Templates Vault from the menu.

3. Check items off as you pack

As you pack, tap each item to mark it complete. Your progress is saved automatically, so you can close the app and come back to it anytime before your trip.

4. Edit your list anytime

You can go back into your trip at any point to:

  • Add or remove items
  • Rename items
  • Reorganize and edit items and categories

Changes save automatically — there’s no separate “save” button to remember.

5. Multiple trips

If you take a similar trip frequently, save your packing list as a template by clicking the template button in the upper-right corner of your trip page.

Past trips can be archived and remain accessible, so you can reuse a list or turn it into a template later. Archives can be found in Settings.

6. Other tips

  • As you get close to your trip (within 5 days), a weather insights icon will appear beside your trip’s name. That will give you the weather for your trip.
  • You can share Onyva in person by clicking the logo at the bottom and having the other person scan the QR code. Or you can click the link to copy it and forward it in a text or email.
  • Sign in using email/password or Google SSO and Onyva will sync across all of your devices.

7. Remember, Onyva is free forever. ONYVA!
