      How these show up in Google Analytics

      Source utm_source
      Where the link lives: linkedin, email, qr. This is the "who sent them" column.
      Medium utm_medium
      The kind of channel. GA4 sorts visits into channel groups using this word, so social lands in Organic Social and email lands in Email. Stick to the dropdown values and your channels stay clean.
      Campaign utm_campaign
      The post or series name. This is what lets a report say which post sent the visits.
      Content utm_content
      Optional. Separates two links inside one campaign.
      Good habits
      Tag links you share outside your own site. Never tag links between pages on youravdept.com, because that restarts the visit and hides where it really came from. Allow up to 48 hours for new tagged visits to appear in GA4 reports.
