Meeting Planner Wellness Playlists

By Michael Harward, Founder, Your AV Department

Three playlists for the three hardest stretches of a show day. Built to get you in, get you through, and get you home.

Free To Use Built For Show Site Clean — No Explicit Tracks No Login Needed
  1. 1Pick Your Moment. Morning ride in, mid-day breather, or after teardown. Each one is built for a different part of the day.
  2. 2Click The Share Icon. Tap the share button on the playlist card to pull up a QR code and link.
  3. 3Send It To Your Team. They scan the code or tap the link to open it in Spotify, no login needed.
  4. 4Take Care Of Yourself. Twelve-hour days are easier with the right soundtrack. That part matters too.

Vibe: Morning Energy

14 Tracks · 51 min

The ride in. Feel-good singalongs from the Beatles to Queen to Earth, Wind & Fire, built to wake up a van full of people before the doors open. Clean enough to play with a client in the back seat.

Vibe: Mid-day Reset

18 Tracks · 1 hr 9 min

The breather between sessions. Warm soul and soft acoustic that brings the blood pressure down without putting you to sleep. Works in headphones or low in the planning office.

Vibe: Evening Wind-down

18 Tracks · 1 hr 8 min

After teardown. Quiet soul, folk, and late-night jazz that drifts further down as the night goes. The sound of a very long day finally being over.
