The Model

Why This Doesn't Cost You Anything

Your AV Department is compensated by our production partners, not by you, after the event.

It works the same way a buyer's agent does in real estate: we represent your interests, negotiate on your behalf, and get paid out of the deal itself, so expert sourcing and project management come at no additional cost to your event budget.

Doesn't that mean the AV production company will just raise the price to cover your fee?
Actually, no, and here's why.

Most production companies carry a team of salaried sales managers earning commission, plus a marketing budget to go find clients like you.

Your AV Department is a channel of revenue for them without any of that overhead attached: no salaries, no marketing spend, just a single fee tied to a project that's already found.

That's a more efficient model of customer acquisition for them, which builds in room to be fair on charges rather than marking it up.

Plus, we encourage a direct relationship between all partners for the long-term. Who doesn't want that?

How It Works

From First Call To Show Day To Debrief

A simple, five-step process: you stay focused on your event, we handle production.

Step 01

Tell Us About Your Event

Room size, dates, audience, and goals: a short conversation is all it takes to get started.

Step 02

We Source & Negotiate

We shortlist vetted production partners, run the RFP, and negotiate scope and pricing on your behalf.

Step 03

We Manage The Details

Timelines, stage plots, and vendor coordination stay on track from advance through load-in.

Step 04

You Focus On Your Event

We're on-site and on call: you run the show, we handle the production behind it.

Step 05

How Did We Do?

We coordinate a debriefing call between us and our production partner to share notes on challenges and what went well. We create a playbook for improvement of your next event.
