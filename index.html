How It Works

From First Call To Show Day To Debrief

A simple, five-step process: you stay focused on your event, we handle production.

Step 01

Tell Us About Your Event

Room size, dates, audience, and goals: a short conversation is all it takes to get started.

Step 02

We Source & Negotiate

We shortlist vetted production partners, run the RFP, and negotiate scope and pricing on your behalf.

Step 03

We Manage The Details

Timelines, stage plots, and vendor coordination stay on track from advance through load-in.

Step 04

You Focus On Your Event

We're on-site and on call: you run the show, we handle the production behind it.

Step 05

How Did We Do?

We coordinate a debriefing call between us and our production partner to share notes on challenges and what went well. We create a playbook for improvement of your next event.
